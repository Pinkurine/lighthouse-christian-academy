Pre-school starts from age 1.5, once your child can walk on their own.

Kurikulum Merdeka, taught closely.

Kurikulum Merdeka is Indonesia's national curriculum, so your child stays on track for any next school.

We teach it the way a family would: closely and personally, with time for each child.

Pendampingan: extra support when it's needed.

Some children need a little extra help, or a different kind of help. We gladly give them personal support.

Pendampingan is given within what our school can provide and within our school rules. Ask our admin and we'll talk about what your child needs.
