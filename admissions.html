Questions parents ask.

Is the trial really free?

Yes. The school tour and the trial day are free, so you and your child can get to know us first.

How much are the school fees?

Fees vary by level. Message our admin on WhatsApp for a consultation and the current fee details.

How long is the voucher valid?

One month after your trial day. After that, registration is still open at the regular admission fee.

What documents do I need?

Our admin sends you the full list of documents and requirements on WhatsApp.

How young can my child start?

Pre-school starts from age 1.5, once your child can walk on their own.

What curriculum do you follow?

Indonesia's Kurikulum Merdeka, with God's Word learned and lived every day.

What are the school hours?

Monday to Friday 07:00 to 14:00, and Saturday 07:00 to 13:00.
